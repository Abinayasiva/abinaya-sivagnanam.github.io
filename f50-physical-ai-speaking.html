From Cloud AI to Physical AI

Abinaya Mettupatti Sivagnanam was invited as a panelist at the F50 Physical AI Summit 2026 at The University of Texas at Dallas for the session “From Cloud AI to Physical AI: Deploying Autonomous Intelligence in Real-World Operations.” The discussion focused on how AI is moving from cloud-based systems into real-world operations where enterprise automation, governance, reliability, security, and human accountability become critical.
